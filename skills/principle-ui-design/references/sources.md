# Fontes

Referencias que embasam a skill. Nao e lido durante a tarefa; use ao revisar ou ampliar a skill.

- [Nielsen Norman Group, 10 Usability Heuristics](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/ten-usability-heuristics/): heuristicas de usabilidade.
- [Don Norman, The Design of Everyday Things](https://jnd.org/books/the-design-of-everyday-things-revised-and-expanded-edition/): affordance, mapeamento, feedback.
- [Laws of UX](https://lawsofux.com/): leis de UX e principios de Gestalt.
- [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): piso de acessibilidade.
- [Apple Human Interface Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/): alvos, interfaces fluidas, spring.
- [Material Design 3](https://m3.material.io/): estados de interacao, alvos, tema escuro.
- [Refactoring UI](https://www.refactoringui.com/): hierarquia, espaco, cor por papel.
- [Butterick, Practical Typography](https://practicaltypography.com/): medida, entrelinha, tracking.
